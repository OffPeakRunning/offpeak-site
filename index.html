Why this exists

Frustration — and a genuine desire to pass it on.
Not a business plan.

Years spent on standardized online coaching, applied to myself, mostly produced fatigue and flat results — plans built for a generic week, indifferent to the one that had actually happened.

At the same time, the same questions kept coming — from family, from friends, from colleagues: what shoes, how to structure a block, how to pace a race. I started answering those, informally, long before Off Peak became anything a person could apply to.

The method itself was built the same way — on thousands of kilometers run, altitude camps in Kenya, dozens of books read, and conversations with coaches worth learning from. And, of course, learning from the many mistakes made along the way.

A real result

Same effort, different plan.

A colleague spent years running the same two hard 10Ks a week on the office treadmill — flat out, no easy days, no progression, no speed work — and signing up for races throughout the year without ever moving the needle. Same time, race after race.

I stepped in and coached him myself — real structure, a plan that actually progressed, mileage built up gradually instead of maxing out twice a week. It's the same approach that became Off Peak.

His 10K went from 50:00 to 45:00 — and he's now closing in on breaking 40:00.

Still deciding

A few more things worth knowing.

What happens on the call before I'm accepted?

A short conversation — your goal, your schedule, what's realistic. It's a two-way check, not an interview you can fail.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No lock-in. It's month to month — you stay because it's working, not because of a contract.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. A message is enough. No forms, no retention calls.

What if my schedule is too unpredictable even for this?

That's exactly the case this was built for. If a week can move at the last minute, the plan is designed to move with it — that's the whole premise, not an edge case.

Do you coach beginners, or is this for competitive runners only?

Both. What matters is commitment to a real goal, not your current level.

Is this remote, or does location matter?

Fully remote. Location doesn't change how it works.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Nothing about your training, your goals, or your schedule is shared, and this isn't a platform where your activity is visible to anyone else.
